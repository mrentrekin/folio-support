What Folio is

Folio is a professional network for people who do meaningful work: your profile and history, a quiet feed of your network, curated business and professional news, podcasts and video, and openings matched privately against what you are open to.

Support

Email info@mindfullcounseling.org and you will hear back. In the app you can also use Settings → Contact Folio, which reaches the same place.

Reporting something

Every post, comment, message and profile has a Report option behind a long press or the menu, and you can block anyone from their profile. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours. If something needs attention faster, email the address above.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account removes your profile, posts, comments, messages, résumé and uploads. It cannot be undone.
