Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

The short version

Folio is a professional network, not an advertising business. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. What you're open to stays private, and your résumé is shared only on the terms you choose.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, and, if you use Sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple provides (which may be a private relay address).
  • Profile: headline, location, about, roles, positions, education, published work, and anything else you add.
  • Résumé: the file you upload, and who you allow to open it.
  • Preferences: what you're open to, your interests and preferred locations.
  • Activity you create: posts, comments, likes, saves, messages, connections, applications, and reports you file.
  • Attachments: photos, videos, PDFs and other files you add to a post, and the preview of any link you share.
  • Organizations: if you create or join one, its details, your role, and your work email domain for verification.
  • Technical: the minimum needed to keep you signed in and the service secure, such as session tokens. Our servers keep short-lived logs that include your IP address and basic device information, used to prevent abuse.

We do not collect your location from your device, your contacts, or advertising identifiers.

Camera, photos and microphone

Folio asks for the camera and microphone only when you choose to take a photo or record a video for a post, and for your photo library only when you pick something from it. Folio can't see your library otherwise, and nothing leaves your phone until you tap Post. When you share a link, your phone fetches that page to build the preview, so the site sees the request the same way your browser would.

How we use it

To run Folio: show your profile, deliver posts and messages to the people you chose, match openings to your preferences on our servers, rank the day's news against your interests, verify organizations, keep the community safe by reviewing reports, and respond when you contact us. We don't use your information for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Your profile is visible to signed-in Folio members.
  • Your posts and comments are visible to you and the people you're connected to. Photos, videos and files you attach follow the post: only those same people can open them, through links that expire.
  • Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation.
  • What you're open to is never shown to anyone, including organizations. Matching happens on our servers.
  • Your résumé follows the setting you choose: No one, Organizations (only those you apply to), or Anyone on Folio.
  • When you apply to an opening, that verified organization sees your application and profile.
  • Blocked members can't see your posts or comments, and can't message or connect with you.

Service providers

We use Supabase to host our database, files and servers (in the United States), and Apple for Sign in with Apple. They process data only to provide those services to us.

Some parts of Folio load content from other companies, and those requests reveal your IP address to them, as any web request does:

  • News stories open on the publisher's own website, under its privacy policy.
  • Podcast listings come from Apple's public podcast directory, and episodes stream from the show's own host.
  • Videos play in YouTube's official player. Google may set cookies and collect data through it, under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy), and YouTube's terms apply (youtube.com/t/terms).

We don't give these companies your Folio account information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect people's safety.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account exists. Delete your account in Settings → Delete account and we delete your profile, posts, comments, likes, saves, applications, preferences, résumé file, the photos, videos and files you attached to posts, and messages you sent, and revoke Folio's access to your Apple ID if you used it. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days. Reports you filed may be kept, without your identity, where needed to keep others safe.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is controlled by rules enforced in our database, so, for example, no organization can read what you're open to even if our app had a bug. No system is perfectly secure; tell us if you find a problem.

Your rights

You can see and edit most of your information in the app, and delete it by deleting your account. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have further rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to complain to a regulator. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising.

Children

Folio is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

We'll tell you in the app about material changes to this policy. Questions or requests: contact us through Settings → Contact Folio in the app, or at info@mindfullcounseling.org.
