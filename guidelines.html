Community Guidelines

Effective September 22, 2026

Build people up

Folio is for real work and real people. Disagree with ideas, not people. Criticism is welcome when it's specific and useful; contempt isn't.

Be who you are

Use your real name. Describe your experience honestly. Only vouch for work you've actually seen. Organizations: post real openings with real terms.

Never

Harass, threaten or demean anyone. Use slurs or hateful language. Post sexual or violent content. Spam, scam, or recruit for anything that asks people for money. Share someone's private information without permission.

If something's wrong

Press and hold a post, comment or message, or use the ••• menu on a profile, to report it or block the person. Reports are reviewed by a person. Breaking these rules can get content removed and accounts banned.
